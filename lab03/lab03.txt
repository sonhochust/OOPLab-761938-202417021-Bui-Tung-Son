Ve phuong thuc toString trong Java(14): Kieu tra ve cua phuong thuc toString luon luon la String. Day la phuong thuc duoc ke thua tu lop cha cao nhat la Object, dung de tao ra mot chuoi dai dien cho thong tin cua doi tuong do.
Phan tich ve thiet ke cac lop tap hop(14): Trong du an AIMS, cac lop dong vai tro la tap hop (aggregates) bao gom Cart, Store va CompactDisc. Lop Cart va Store chua mot danh sach cac doi tuong Media, trong khi CompactDisc lai chua mot danh sach cac doi tuong Track. De dam bao chuong trinh chay on dinh, cac ham khoi tao cua cac lop nay deu phai thuc hien viec khoi tao danh sach (vi du nhu ArrayList) ngay tu dau de tranh loi truy cap vao doi tuong rong.
Xu ly logic trong phuong thuc equals(15): Khi thuc hien ghi de phuong thuc equals, neu doi tuong truyen vao khong phai la mot thuc the cua lop Media, he thong nen tra ve gia tri false ngay lap tuc. Viec su dung toan tu instanceof truoc khi ep kieu la rat quan trong de dam bao tinh an toan cho ma nguon, tranh cac loi xung dot kieu du lieu khi so sanh.
Co che da hinh khi goi toString(16): Trong phan thuc hanh duyet danh sach Media, hien tuong xay ra chinh la da hinh tai thoi diem chay. Du chung ta goi phuong thuc toString tren mot tham chieu kieu Media, nhung Java se tu dong tim va thuc thi phien ban toString cu the cua lop con nhu Book, CD hoac DVD tuy thuoc vao doi tuong thuc te dang nam trong bo nho. Dieu nay cho phep moi loai san pham hien thi thong tin theo cach rieng biet cua no.
Uu diem va vai tro cua da hinh va ke thua(muc doc them): - Da hinh mang lai su linh hoat toi da cho he thong, cho phep mot doan ma co the lam viec voi nhieu loai doi tuong khac nhau ma khong can biet chinh xac kieu cu the cua chung. Ke thua dong vai tro la nen tang de dat duoc da hinh bang cach thiet lap moi quan he "la mot" giua cac lop, cho phep lop con ke thua cau truc va ghi de hanh vi tu lop cha.
-Su khac biet cot loi nam o muc dich su dung: Ke thua tap trung vao viec tai su dung ma nguon va mo rong thuoc tinh, trong khi da hinh tap trung vao viec cung cap mot giao dien chung cho cac hanh vi khac nhau. Ke thua la phuong tien, con da hinh la kha nang xu ly linh hoat dua tren phuong tien do.
